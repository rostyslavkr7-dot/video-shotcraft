## Monetization for a Germany-based creator
- **TikTok Creator Rewards** — Germany is one of the 8 eligible countries. You need 18+, 10,000 followers,
  100,000 views in the last 30 days, and videos **longer than 1 minute** (our episodes: 62.8 s and 67.8 s).
  It must be a **personal account**: don't switch to a business account, which is excluded from the program.
  Views only pay from eligible countries, which is why the English audience (US/UK) works best.
- **YouTube Shorts (YPP)** — 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days for ad revenue share.
  Upload the same videos as Shorts.
- **Instagram Reels** — cross-post for reach.
- On every platform, mark the videos as AI-generated: the "AI-generated content" label on TikTok,
  "Altered or synthetic content" on YouTube.

## First steps
1. Register TikTok and YouTube with the same handle; the avatar and bio are above.
2. Warm up the TikTok account for 3–5 days: 15–20 min a day watching and liking AI/tech/study content in English.
3. Post 1 video a day. For the US audience, 18:00–21:00 ET (00:00–03:00 Berlin): TikTok's scheduler handles this.
   Or post at 13:00–15:00 Berlin = morning in the US / evening in the UK.
4. Pin episode 1.
