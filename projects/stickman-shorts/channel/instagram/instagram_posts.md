# Instagram Reels: Stickstruck

The video file is the same as for TikTok/YouTube (1080×1920, 9:16, 60–68 s). Covers are in `covers/`
(made by `make_covers.py`; text sits inside the centre 1080×1350 band the profile grid shows).
Instagram allows at most 5 hashtags per post: use exactly these.

## Before the first upload (once)
- Settings → Data usage and media quality → **Upload at highest quality** = on (otherwise Reels get compressed).
- Professional account → Creator is fine for Reels; the TikTok "personal account" rule does not apply here.

## Every upload
- Cover: **Edit cover → Add from camera roll** → the matching `covers/*.jpg`. Check the 4:5 grid preview.
- **AI label** (Advanced settings → "Add AI info") = on.
- Original audio name: rename "Original audio" to `Stickstruck`.
- Post 14:00–16:00 Berlin, same day as TikTok/YouTube or one day later.
