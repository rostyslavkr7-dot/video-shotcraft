Every thirty minutes, he threw away his entire conversation with the AI.
His thesis is due in the morning. Claude is coding with him, everything finally works… and he copies five lines — and opens a brand-new, empty chat.
His roommate nearly drops his pizza: “You just threw away three hours of work!”
The roommate has been in the same chat since lunch. A thousand messages, the whole history right there — why start over?
But by 2 a.m., his replies start crawling. And then: “Usage limit reached. Come back in five hours.”
The thesis is due at eight.
See, before every single reply, Claude rereads the entire chat from the very beginning. Imagine having to reread a whole book from page one before writing each new sentence.
The longer the chat, the more of your limit every message eats.
And those five lines? A summary: what’s done, and what’s next. Five lines instead of a thousand messages.
So at eight a.m., one of them hands in his thesis.
And the other is explaining to the committee that his AI ran out.
